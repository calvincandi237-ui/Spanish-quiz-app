Privacy Policy — Spanish Quiz Last updated: 2026 Spanish Quiz is an app for learning Spanish. We take your privacy seriously, so this policy is very short: there is almost nothing for the app to tell you about. What Data the App Collects None. The app does not collect or send to a server: your name, email address, or any personal information; your location; contacts, photos, or files from your device; data for advertising or analytics. What Is Stored on Your Device Your progress (score, level, coins, daily streak, exam attempt history, and word review progress) is stored only locally on your device, in the browser's storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or to any third party. If you delete the app or clear your browser data, this progress will be permanently deleted. Accounts and Registration You can use the app without registering. We do not create or store user accounts. Third Parties The app does not use analytics services, advertising networks, or trackers. The only external request is to load the user interface styling library (Tailwind CSS) from a public CDN. This request does not transmit any personal data about you. Children The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children. Changes to This Policy If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
